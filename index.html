About

I'm a PhD student at the Chinese University of Hong Kong (Shenzhen). I'm in particular interested in theoretical aspects of high-energy physics and their mathematical structures.

Research Interests

  • Perturbative QCD

Projects

  • Gauge group digitization: Hamiltonian formulations of lattice gauge theories promises numerous advantages over the common lattice Monte Carlo methods. However, Performing such simulations will require a large scale of the quantum computers in terms of memory and circuit depth. Thus, it is therefore essential to explore the possibilities of reducing the requirement of quantum resources. The digitization of the subgroups of gauge groups have been studied detailedly. To overcome obstructions in simulations, especially the freezing occurrence, we examine subsets of gauge groups.
  • Hadronic structures: The Deeply-Virtual Compton Scattering (DVCS) has been one of the most efficient process that give access to generalized parton distributions (GPDs). We calculate the higher order power corrections to this process, and also the double DVCS.
